CoreGuard Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 2026 · Package: com.coldboar.coreguard

Our Commitment

CoreGuard is designed to protect your privacy, not exploit it. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Security features are intended to run on your device.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Name, email, or contact information
  • Device advertising IDs
  • Location data
  • Browsing history
  • Biometric templates
  • Payment card details (handled by Google Play)

On-Device Processing

Security scans, threat analysis, and compliance scoring run locally. Scan results are stored in the app’s private storage and are not uploaded to a CoreGuard backend.

Threat Signature Updates & Quilla Intel Network

CoreGuard may optionally fetch public threat-signature lists (IOC feeds) and defensive security knowledge over HTTPS from open sources such as the Amnesty International Security Lab / mvt-project, CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities, and MISP Android malware galaxy briefs. These requests are anonymous and do not include account identifiers. Quilla Research sync does not send personal scan results off-device.

Privacy Shield (VPN)

Privacy Shield uses Android’s VPN API on-device to filter DNS against a blocklist of known surveillance/tracker domains. Blocked domains receive a local NXDOMAIN-style response. Allowed DNS queries are forwarded to your device’s system resolver (or a public upstream resolver such as 8.8.8.8 if none is available). This is DNS filtering, not full traffic inspection. App traffic payloads are not routed through CoreGuard servers, and CoreGuard does not store traffic content.

Permissions

  • INTERNET — optional IOC feed refresh; on-device VPN DNS forwarding; local Frida port probes
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE / SPECIAL_USE — Privacy Shield VPN notification service
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — VPN status notification
  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES — compare installed packages to spyware package indicators on-device
  • Notification access (optional) — Scam Guard may use Android’s Notification Listener API when you explicitly enable CoreGuard under system Notification access. It inspects notification text on-device for smishing / scam URL heuristics. Notification content is not uploaded to a CoreGuard backend and is not used for advertising.

Scam Guard (optional Notification Listener)

Scam Guard’s live interception is opt-in. Until you grant Notification access in Android Settings, CoreGuard does not read other apps’ notifications. When enabled, processing stays on-device; CoreGuard does not sell or share notification contents.

Subscriptions & Billing

Premium subscriptions use Google Play Billing. CoreGuard does not receive your payment card details. Entitlement is determined from Play purchase state on the device.

Children’s Privacy

CoreGuard is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions: open an issue or discussion on the CoreGuard-Android GitHub repository or use the support contact on the Play Store listing.

CoreGuard is an independent project and is not affiliated with Amnesty International. Privacy signatures may be sourced from Amnesty International Security Lab / mvt-project materials.
